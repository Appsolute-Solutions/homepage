Nudge privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Nudge is a reminder app that reminds you when you arrive at or leave places. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: it has no accounts and no ads. Your reminders stay on your phone, and your location is only sent to OpenStreetMap services to find places and routes. Nudge sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics, which never include your reminders, places or location. The developer has no server and never receives your reminders or location.

Location

Nudge uses your phone's location to know when you arrive at or leave the places in your reminders, including when the app is closed or not in use. This is the core of the app; without it, reminders can't fire.

  • Checking whether you've reached a place happens on your phone, using Google Play services' geofencing and, for the few minutes you're close to a reminder, GPS. A notification shows while GPS is on.
  • To find places, addresses and driving routes, Nudge sends location details to services built on OpenStreetMap: Photon (run by komoot), Nominatim, Overpass and the OSRM routing service. What's sent depends on what you're doing:
    • Finding nearby places: a box around your position, or around your route or a suburb you named, plus the kind of place or shop name. For shop names this includes the box's centre point, which can be your position.
    • "Right here" and saving where you are: your exact position, to look up its address.
    • "On the way to" reminders: your exact starting point and destination.
    • Searching for an address or suburb: what you typed, and a box around you.
  • Some of this happens in the background. When you move out of the area Nudge last searched, it searches the new area so reminders like "any petrol station" keep working.
  • The task in your reminder ("buy milk") isn't sent, only the place. These services see what was requested and your IP address, and are run under their own policies (https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy, https://www.komoot.com/privacy).

Microphone and speech

When you tap the microphone, your phone's speech recognition service (Google's on most phones) turns what you say into text. Nudge only receives the text. It does not record or store audio.

Your reminders and places

Reminders, saved places and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Android's own backup may include them in your Google account backup if you've turned that on.

Purchases

Nudge Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; Nudge only learns whether your account has an active Pro purchase.

Usage statistics

Nudge uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the app is used, so we can fix what's confusing and decide what to build next. It records which screens are opened (including each setup step), when a reminder is saved, goes off, is put off or is marked done, and when the Nudge Pro screen opens and a purchase starts, completes, is cancelled or fails. Each event only says what kind of thing happened, for example "a reminder for a saved place, on arrival, made by voice". Events never include what a reminder says, place or shop names, addresses, what you searched for, or your location.

Google Analytics also collects some information itself: a random app instance ID, your phone model, Android version, app version, language, sessions and time spent in the app, and the country or region Google works out from your IP address. Nudge turns off collection of the advertising ID and does not have the advertising ID permission. Google processes this data for Appsolute Dev under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Uninstalling Nudge or clearing its data resets the app instance ID.

What Nudge does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling your data, or sharing it beyond the services named above.
