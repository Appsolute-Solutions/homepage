Paper Parade privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Paper Parade ("the game") is published by Appsolute Dev, a trading name of Appsolute Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information in the game. The short version: Paper Parade has no account, no ads and no server of ours that holds your data. Your progress stays on your phone. If usage statistics are on, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics; you can turn this off at any time.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com

What the game stores, and where

  • your progress: the levels you have reached and the stars you have won on each, how many tries you have started on each level, your coins, lives and boosters, which tips you have already seen, a count of levels started, won and lost, and your settings;
  • if you buy coins, the purchase token Google Play gives each purchase, and whether you have bought the starter bundle (see Purchases);
  • your age band (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) and your answer on usage statistics.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the game's private storage, together with a backup copy the game uses if its saved data is ever damaged. None of it is sent to us. There are no accounts, advertising or crash-reporting services in the game, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, location or advertising ID.

The game is set to stay out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. Because we never receive it, we cannot view or recover it for you.

Age screen

Paper Parade is made for adults and is not directed at children. When you first open the game it asks what year you were born. It uses the year only to work out an age band and then discards it: only the band is saved on your device. If the answer shows you could be under 13, the game does not open, and we collect nothing from you. Players aged 13 to 17 can use the game with a parent or guardian's permission and receive no personalised content or offers.

This check is based on what you tell it. The result is stored on your device, so reinstalling the game or clearing its data lets it ask again. If you believe a child has been using Paper Parade, contact us at support@appsolutedev.com.

Usage statistics

If usage statistics are on, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service, so we can see how the game is played and which levels are too hard or too easy. The events cover the game being opened, which screen is open, a level starting and ending (with the level number, the try number, whether it was won and how many moves were left), a booster being used, the offer of extra moves and whether it was taken (with its price in coins), the coin shop being opened, a purchase being started and how it ended (bought, pending, cancelled or failed, with the name of the pack), and a setting being changed. They carry game values only. They do not include your age band or anything that identifies you by name.

Google Analytics also collects some information itself: a random app instance ID, your phone model, Android version, app version, language, sessions and time played, and the country or region Google works out from your IP address. Collection of the advertising ID and Android ID is turned off, the game has no ads, and no statistics are used for advertising or personalised offers. Google processes this information for us under the Google Privacy Policy and may process it in other countries, including the United States.

Usage statistics are off until you pass the age screen, and never on if the game is locked because you could be under 13. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the game can't tell your region, they stay off unless you say yes when the game asks. Elsewhere they are on unless you turn them off. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, Privacy, Usage statistics; turning it off stops anything more being sent. While the game is running, a short list of recent game events is also kept in memory on your phone; it is never saved and is gone when the game closes.

Purchases

Paper Parade is free to play. You can also buy coin packs, and once a starter bundle of coins and boosters, through Google Play Billing. Google Play is the payment processor: you pay with your Google account under Google's terms, and we never see your card or payment details. Paper Parade has no purchase server and uses no other purchase service.

The game reads Google Play's record of your purchases on your device. It keeps each purchase's token in your saved game so that a purchase is added once, and so that an interrupted or pending payment is added when Google Play confirms it. Google also gives us order records in our developer account (for example product, price, country and order number) for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If usage statistics are on, the game sends the shop and purchase events listed under Usage statistics, which name the pack but carry no payment details, and Google Analytics may record a completed purchase (the pack, its price and currency) itself. The game connects to Google Play Billing only after the age screen, and never if the game is locked because you could be under 13. Google may process this information in other countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards.

Permissions

Paper Parade asks for no camera, photo, location, contacts or microphone access. Its Android manifest includes the billing permission, used to buy coins through Google Play, the vibration permission for the small taps of feedback you can switch off in Settings, and network access to reach Google Analytics if usage statistics are on. The Settings screen has links to this policy and our terms, which open in your browser.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on your phone until you delete it. In Settings, Privacy, Delete my data you can erase everything the game stores on your device, including your progress, coins, lives, boosters, settings and your privacy choices. Coins and boosters you bought and have not used are erased too, and we cannot restore them, because your saved game never reaches us. If the game locked you out as under 13, that lock is removed too. Deleting also resets the Google Analytics app instance ID on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored.

Usage statistics already sent to Google Analytics are kept by Google for the data retention period set in our Google Analytics account and then deleted. They are not linked to your name, so to ask us to delete them, email support@appsolutedev.com and we will help where we can.

Security

Your progress is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and device encryption, and by the operating system keeping each app's storage private. Usage statistics travel to Google over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, POPIA in South Africa, the Australian Privacy Act, PIPEDA in Canada or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the game keeps your progress on your phone and statistics are anonymous, most of these rights are exercised in the game itself (Settings, Privacy). For anything else, email support@appsolutedev.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it at https://appsolutedev.com/paperparade/privacy.html and change the effective date above. If a change affects what the game collects, we will tell you in the game before it applies.
