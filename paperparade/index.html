Paper Parade

A relaxing match-3 puzzle game for Android

Swap neighbouring paper pieces to make rows of three. Bigger matches fold into scissors, pinwheels, paper cranes and confetti poppers. Peel gift wrap, open parcels and free taped pieces across 60 levels.

  • Short levels with clear goals and no timers.
  • Gentle teaching: the first levels show you what to do.
  • Free to play, no ads, nothing random for sale.

Paper Parade is coming to Google Play.
