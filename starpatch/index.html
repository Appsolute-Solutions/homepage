Starpatch

A Star Battle logic puzzle for Android

One star in every row, every column and every coloured patch, and no two stars may touch, not even at a corner. Every board has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone.

  • A new daily puzzle every day, from a gentle 6x6 on Monday to a full 10x10 on Sunday.
  • Unlimited boards from 5x5 to 10x10 in three difficulties.
  • Hints that explain the next step instead of just showing it.
  • No ads, no account, and your progress stays on your phone.

Starpatch is coming to Google Play.
