Clearday privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Clearday is published by Appsolute Dev (Appsolute Solutions), South Africa. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Clearday app on Android or iOS. The short version: your habits, streaks, slips, cravings, check-ins and journal never leave your phone.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com
Web version: https://appsolutedev.com/clearday/privacy.html

1. What Clearday stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • the habits you track, your quit date, cut-down plan, daily cost and time, and your reasons
  • slips (date, time, optional note, and whether your streak was reset)
  • cravings logged through the SOS screen (time, strength, triggers, outcome)
  • daily check-ins (mood, craving level, triggers, pledge) and cut-down counts
  • journal entries and their mood
  • settings: reminder times, currency, app lock on or off, and the name and phone number of a person you choose to call

We have no servers that receive this information, no account system, and no way to see it. We cannot recover it for you if your phone is lost or reset.

2. What Clearday does not do

  • No account, sign-up, email or password.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No remote logging, no cloud sync and no backups made by us.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. The "person to call" is typed in by you.

3. Purchases (the one exception)

Clearday is free to use, with an optional paid upgrade called Clearday Pro. If you buy Pro:

  • Google Play or the Apple App Store processes the payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Our purchase service, RevenueCat, Inc., receives a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase receipt and basic technical details needed to validate it (such as app version, platform and store country). It uses this only to confirm your purchase and unlock Pro, including after a restore. RevenueCat never receives your habits, entries or any other content from the app. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

When the app starts it may contact RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. If you never buy Pro, the only information sent is the anonymous app user ID and technical details needed to answer that check.

4. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Clearday schedules local notifications on your phone (daily check-in, morning pledge, milestones). They are created on the device, no push service or server is involved, and the text never says what you are quitting.

5. App lock

If you turn on app lock (Pro), Clearday asks your phone's operating system to confirm it is you with Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode. Clearday never receives or stores your biometric data or passcode; it only learns whether the check succeeded.

6. Sharing and export

  • Sharing a milestone creates a short text that you send through your phone's share sheet, to whoever you choose. It does not say what you are quitting.
  • CSV export (Pro) creates a file in the app's temporary storage and opens the share sheet so you can save or send it. Where it goes is your choice. Old export files are deleted from temporary storage the next time the app opens.
  • Links to helplines, our terms and this policy open in your browser and are subject to those websites' policies.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Settings, Data, "Delete all data" erases everything Clearday stores on the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat (for example to request deletion of the anonymous purchase record), email support@appsolutedev.com. We will respond within 30 days, in line with POPIA (South Africa), the GDPR and similar laws where they apply.

8. Health information

Clearday is a self-help tracker. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and does not replace professional care. Health timelines are general public-health information summarised from sources such as the WHO, CDC and NHS, and are not medical advice. If you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor before stopping suddenly: alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous.

9. Children

Clearday is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

10. Security

Your data stays inside the app's private storage on your phone, protected by your device's own security. For extra protection, use a screen lock on your phone and turn on Clearday's app lock.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version at https://appsolutedev.com/clearday/privacy.html. If a change ever affected how your data is handled, we would explain it in the app before it applied.
