Floodplain privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Floodplain ("the game") is published by Appsolute Dev, a trading name of Appsolute Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information in the game. The short version: Floodplain has no account, no ads and no server of ours that holds your data. Your progress stays on your phone, and in this version nothing is sent to us.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com

What the game stores, and where

  • your progress, coins, boosters, lives, daily and seasonal progress, and settings;
  • your age band (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) and your answer on usage statistics;
  • a record of each purchase made through Google Play (the product, Google Play order number, purchase token and time), so that each purchase is delivered once.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the game's private storage. Nothing is sent to us. There are no accounts, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services in the game, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or advertising ID.

Your data is included in your phone's own backups only if your backup settings include app data. Because we never receive it, we cannot view or recover it for you.

Age screen

Floodplain is made for adults and is not directed at children. When you first open the game it asks what year you were born. It uses the year only to work out an age band and then discards it: only the band is saved on your device. If the answer shows you could be under 13, the game does not open, and we collect nothing from you. Players aged 13 to 17 can use the game with a parent or guardian's permission and receive no personalised content or offers.

This check is based on what you tell it. The result is stored on your device, so reinstalling the game or clearing its data lets it ask again. If you believe a child has been using Floodplain, contact us at support@appsolutedev.com.

Usage statistics

The game has a Usage statistics switch in Settings, Privacy, and in some regions it asks you about it on first launch. In this version no statistics service is connected, so nothing about how you play is sent to us or anyone else, whatever the switch says. While the game is running, a short list of recent game events (such as a level starting or ending) is kept in memory on your device for debugging and is gone when the game closes.

Purchases

If you buy coins or other items, the purchase goes through Google Play Billing. Google Play is the payment processor: you pay with your Google account under Google's terms, and we never see your card or payment details. Floodplain has no purchase server and uses no other purchase service.

The game reads Google Play's record of your purchases on your device. It keeps the order number and purchase token in your saved game so that a purchase is delivered once, so that an interrupted or pending payment is delivered when Google Play confirms it, and so that a season Almanac you bought is unlocked again after a reinstall. Google also gives us order records in our developer account (for example product, price, country and order number) for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The game connects to Google Play Billing only after the age screen, and never if the game is locked because you could be under 13. Google may process this information in other countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards.

Permissions

Floodplain asks for no camera, photo, location, contacts or microphone access. It uses the billing permission so you can buy items through Google Play. The Settings screen has links to this policy and our terms, which open in your browser.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on your phone until you delete it. In Settings, Privacy, Delete my data you can erase everything the game stores on your device, including progress, coins, boosters, settings and your privacy choices. Coins and boosters you bought are removed too and are not given back. If the game locked you out as under 13, that lock is kept. You can also uninstall the game, which removes its data from the device.

Records of past purchases stay with Google Play and in our Google Play developer account, because Google and we must keep order records for tax and accounting. Season Almanacs you bought belong to your Google account and unlock again once Google Play confirms them. To ask about any of this, email support@appsolutedev.com.

Security

Your progress is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and device encryption, and by the operating system keeping each app's storage private. Purchases are handled by Google Play over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, POPIA in South Africa, the Australian Privacy Act, PIPEDA in Canada or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to complain to a regulator. Because the game's data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the game. For anything else, contact us at support@appsolutedev.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it at https://appsolutedev.com/floodplain/privacy.html and change the effective date above. If a change affects what information is collected, we will tell you in the game before it applies.
