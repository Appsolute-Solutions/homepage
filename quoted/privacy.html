Quoted privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Quoted ("the app") is published by Appsolute Dev, a trading name of Appsolute Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information in the app. The short version: your business records stay on your phone, we do not run servers that receive them, and we cannot see them.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com

What the app stores, and where

When you use Quoted you may enter:

  • your business details (name, trade, contact details, address, tax and registration numbers, payment instructions and payment link, logo, brand colour);
  • your clients' details (name, company, phone, email, address, tax number, private notes);
  • catalogue items, quotes, invoices, payments, notes and terms;
  • client signatures captured on the screen, and photos of jobs you attach.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not have accounts, a cloud database, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services in the app.

Because we never receive this data, we cannot view, recover or delete it for you. Keep a backup with Settings > Export all data.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Sending a document or export. When you tap Send, send a reminder or export data, the app creates a PDF, CSV or JSON file and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes to the app or person you pick (for example WhatsApp, email or a cloud drive) and is then handled under that service's terms. We do not receive a copy.
  • Links you tap. Opening a payment link, our website, or a phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email link hands over to the relevant app on your phone.

Purchases

Quoted Pro is sold through Google Play (and the App Store on iOS). We never see your card or payment details; the store handles payment.

To unlock Pro and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription service provider. The RevenueCat software in the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous app user ID generated for this install, basic device and app information (such as the operating system, app version and store country) and the records of your purchases. RevenueCat uses this only to provide the subscription service to us. No business or client data from the app is sent. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

RevenueCat may process this information in the United States. We rely on its contractual safeguards for those transfers.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional): to take job photos when you tap Take a photo. Photos are saved inside the app.
  • Photos: when you choose a logo or a job photo, the system photo picker gives the app only the image you select. The app cannot browse your gallery.
  • Notifications (optional, Pro): reminders to you when an invoice becomes overdue or a recurring invoice is due. They are scheduled on your phone; there are no push notifications and nothing is sent to a server.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual documents, clients and items, erase everything with Settings > Erase all data, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from the device.

To ask about or request deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat, email support@appsolutedev.com from the account you bought with and we will handle it with RevenueCat. Store purchase history itself is kept by Google or Apple under their policies.

Security

Your records are protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and device encryption, and by the operating system keeping each app's storage private. Generated PDFs block scripts and remote content. Please use a screen lock, because anyone who can open your phone can open the app.

Children

Quoted is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under POPIA in South Africa, the GDPR in the UK and EU, or Australian, New Zealand, Canadian or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to complain to a regulator. Because the app's data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app. For anything else, including the purchase data described above, contact us at support@appsolutedev.com.

If you enter your clients' personal information, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep it, as you would with any record book.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it at https://appsolutedev.com/quoted/privacy.html and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
