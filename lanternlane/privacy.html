Lantern Lane Café privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Lantern Lane Café ("the game") is published by Appsolute Dev, a trading name of Appsolute Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information in the game. The short version: Lantern Lane Café has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of ours that holds your data. Your café and your progress stay on your phone, and the game sends nothing to us.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com

What the game stores, and where

  • your café and your progress: your café level, coins and gems, the decor you own and where you have placed it, your recipes, machines and staff, your regulars' stories and the choices you made in them, goals, daily tasks, achievements, catering orders and festival progress, and when you last played, so the game can work out what your staff earned while you were away;
  • the name you give your café, the name you type for yourself and your character's look;
  • your settings: sound effects, music, vibration, recipe hints and reduced motion;
  • if you buy gems, the purchase token Google Play gives each purchase, and whether you have bought the starter bundle (see Purchases);
  • your age band (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over).

All of this is stored only on your device, in the game's private storage, together with a backup copy the game uses if its saved data is ever damaged. None of it is sent to us, and the names you type never leave your phone. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services in the game, and it does not ask for your email, contacts, photos, microphone, location or advertising ID. There are no other players: the rival cafés in festivals are made up by the game, so nothing about you or your café is shown to anyone else.

The game is set to stay out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your café is not copied to your Google account. Because we never receive it, we cannot view or recover it for you.

Age screen

Lantern Lane Café is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children. When you first open the game it asks what year you were born. It uses the year only to work out an age band and then discards it: only the band is saved on your device. If the answer shows you could be under 13, the game does not open, and it saves only that band so that it stays locked on that phone. Players aged 13 to 17 can play with a parent or guardian's permission. The game shows no personalised content or offers to anyone.

This check is based on what you tell it. The result is stored on your device. Delete my data keeps it, so the game does not ask again; uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings removes it, and the game then asks again. If you believe a child has been using Lantern Lane Café, contact us at support@appsolutedev.com.

Purchases

Lantern Lane Café is free to play. You can also buy gem packs, and once per saved game a starter bundle of gems, coins and decor, through Google Play Billing. Google Play is the payment processor: you pay with your Google account under Google's terms, and we never see your card or payment details. Lantern Lane Café has no purchase server and uses no other purchase service.

The game reads Google Play's record of your purchases on your device. It keeps each purchase's token in your saved game so that a purchase is added once, and so that an interrupted or pending payment is added when Google Play confirms it. Google also gives us order records in our developer account (for example product, price, country and order number) for accounting, refunds and fraud prevention. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The game connects to Google Play Billing only after the age screen, and never if the game is locked because you could be under 13. Google may process this information in other countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards.

Permissions

Lantern Lane Café asks for no camera, photo, location, contacts, microphone or notification access. Its Android manifest includes the billing permission, used to buy gems through Google Play, the vibration permission for the light buzz you can switch off in Settings, and network access, which the app framework includes as standard. The game makes no internet connections of its own: purchases go through the Google Play app on your phone. The Settings screen has links to this policy and our terms, which open in your browser.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on your phone until you delete it. In Settings, Privacy, Delete my data you can erase your saved game and its backup copy: your café, progress, coins, gems, decor, recipes, staff, stories, names, settings and purchase tokens. Gems, coins and decor you bought are erased too, and we cannot restore them, because your saved game never reaches us. The game then starts a fresh café. Your age band is kept, so the game does not ask your year of birth again. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything it stored, including the age band.

Records of past purchases stay with Google Play and in our Google Play developer account, because Google and we must keep order records for tax and accounting. To ask about any of this, email support@appsolutedev.com.

Security

Your café is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and device encryption, and by the operating system keeping each app's storage private. Purchases are handled by Google Play over its own encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, POPIA in South Africa, the Australian Privacy Act, PIPEDA in Canada or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the game keeps everything on your phone and sends nothing to us, you can exercise most of these in the game itself, with Settings, Privacy, Delete my data. For anything else, including the order records Google Play gives us, email support@appsolutedev.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it at https://appsolutedev.com/lanternlane/privacy.html and change the effective date above. If a change affects what the game collects, we will tell you in the game before it applies.
