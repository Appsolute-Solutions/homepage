What Glidepath does

Glidepath helps you keep a private record of your GLP-1 medication. You can log injections or pills, rotate injection sites, track side effects, weight, protein, water and fibre, get dose reminders, and create a report to bring to your doctor. Some features are free and others need Glidepath Pro; the app shows which before you buy.

Not a medical device or medical advice

  • Glidepath is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or dosing recommendations, and it does not replace your doctor, pharmacist or other healthcare professional.
  • You enter the dose your prescriber gave you. Glidepath never calculates, suggests or converts doses.
  • The medication level chart is an estimate for illustration only. It is based on published half-lives and a standard absorption model, not on measurements of your body. Never use it to decide when to take a dose or how much to take.
  • Goals for protein, water and fibre are yours to set. Check them with your clinician.
  • Reminders can arrive late or not at all, for example if notifications are turned off, your phone is off, or you have not opened the app for about three weeks. Do not rely on Glidepath as your only reminder.
  • Reports and exports only contain what you logged. Check them before you share them.
  • Always follow your prescriber's instructions. If you are worried about a side effect, contact your doctor. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
  • Medication brand names shown in the app belong to their owners. Glidepath is not made by, connected with or endorsed by any medication maker.
