Glidepath privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Glidepath is published by Appsolute Dev (Appsolute Solutions), South Africa. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Glidepath app for Android and iOS. Contact: support@appsolutedev.com.

The short version

  • Glidepath has no account and no servers that hold your data. Everything you log stays on your device.
  • We (the developer) do not collect, receive, sell or share your health information, photos or anything else you enter.
  • Purchases of Glidepath Pro are handled by Google Play or the App Store and confirmed through our subscription provider, RevenueCat. They receive purchase information, never your health logs.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

Information stored on your device

When you use Glidepath, the following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Medications you add: name, form, prescribed dose and unit, schedule, start date, optional half-life, and optional doses on hand.
  • Doses you log: date and time, amount, injection site, whether it was skipped, and notes.
  • Weights, optional height, starting and goal weight, and body measurements.
  • Daily logs: protein, water and fibre amounts, side effects and their severity, food noise, appetite and energy ratings, and day notes.
  • Progress photos you choose to add. These are copied into the app's private folder; they are not added to your gallery.
  • Your settings, such as units, goals and reminder preferences.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see "Reports and exports"). It is protected by your phone's own security, such as the screen lock and device encryption. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see it.

Camera and photos

Glidepath asks for camera access only when you tap "Take a photo" for a progress photo. When you choose an existing photo, Android's system photo picker (or the iOS picker) gives the app only the photo you select. Glidepath does not access the rest of your photo library and never uploads photos.

Notifications

Reminders (dose, evening-before, daily check-in and refill reminders) are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. No reminder content is sent to us or to any push-notification service. By default, reminders don't show your medication's name on the lock screen; you can change this in Settings.

Reports and exports

If you create a doctor report (PDF) or export your data (CSV), the file is created on your device and handed to your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes, for example email, a messaging app or cloud storage. Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or person you shared it with, under their own terms. Glidepath keeps no copy and the optional name you type on a report is not saved.

Purchases (Glidepath Pro)

Payments are processed by Google Play (Android) or Apple's App Store (iOS). We never see your payment card details.

To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription management service acting on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not linked to your name or email by us),
  • your purchase and subscription history for Glidepath (product, price, dates, store transaction identifiers),
  • basic technical information sent with any internet request, such as IP address, device model, operating system and app version, and store country.

RevenueCat does not receive anything you log in Glidepath. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google Play's and Apple's handling of purchases is covered by their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, login or user profile on our side.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information.
  • No location tracking and no access to contacts.

Children

Glidepath is intended for adults aged 18 and over who are taking prescribed medication. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can erase everything at any time in Settings, Delete all data, which removes all medications, doses, weights, check-ins, measurements and photos, and cancels reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you; export a CSV first if you want a copy.

Device backups: if your phone's own backup service (for example Google or iCloud device backup) is turned on, the operating system may include app data in your personal backup, under that service's terms and your account.

To ask RevenueCat-held purchase records to be deleted, email support@appsolutedev.com from any address and include the app user ID shown to RevenueCat support or your store order number; we will pass the request on.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under POPIA in South Africa, the GDPR in the UK and EU, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to processing. Since your Glidepath data is held only by you on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, or any question about this policy, contact support@appsolutedev.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in South Africa, the Information Regulator).

Not medical advice

Glidepath is a personal logging tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or dosing recommendations. The medication-level chart is an estimate for illustration only. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at https://appsolutedev.com/glidepath/privacy.html. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
