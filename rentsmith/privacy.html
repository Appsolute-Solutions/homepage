Rentsmith privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Rentsmith ("the app") is published by Appsolute Dev, a trading name of Appsolute Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information in the app. The short version: Rentsmith has no account and no server of ours, everything you enter stays on your phone, and we cannot see your properties, tenants, payments, expenses, reminders or photos.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com

What the app stores, and where

When you use Rentsmith you may enter:

  • properties: nicknames, addresses and your ownership share;
  • tenancies: tenant names, the phone numbers and email addresses you type in, dates, rent, deposit amounts, deposit scheme names and references, and notes;
  • rent payments, expenses (with suppliers and notes) and compliance reminders;
  • photos you choose to attach, such as receipts, certificates and tenancy documents;
  • your settings, such as country, currency, notification time and the name you use in rent reminder messages.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Photos are copied into that storage and resized; they are never added to your gallery or uploaded. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, cloud database, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services in the app.

Your data is included in your phone's own backups only if your backup settings include app data. Because we never receive it, we cannot view, recover or delete it for you. Keep a copy with Settings > Export all data.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Sharing a report, export or message. When you share a report PDF, a CSV, a JSON export or a rent reminder message, the app opens your phone's share sheet. It goes to the app or person you pick (for example email or WhatsApp) and is then handled under that service's terms. Rentsmith never sends anything by itself, and we do not receive a copy.
  • Links you tap. Tapping a tenant's phone number or email address, or our website, hands over to the relevant app on your phone.

Purchases

Rentsmith Pro is sold through Google Play (and the App Store on iOS). We never see your card or payment details; the store handles payment.

To unlock Pro and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription service provider. The RevenueCat software in the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous app user ID generated for this install, basic device and app information (such as the operating system, app version and store country) and the records of your purchases. RevenueCat uses this only to provide the subscription service to us. No property, tenant or financial records from the app are sent. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat may process this information in the United States. We rely on its contractual safeguards for those transfers.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional): only when you tap Camera to photograph a receipt, certificate or document. Photos are saved inside the app.
  • Photos: when you choose a photo, the system photo picker gives the app only the image you select. The app cannot browse your gallery.
  • Notifications (optional): reminders 30, 7 and 1 days before a deadline. They are scheduled on your phone; there are no push notifications and nothing is sent to a server.
  • Billing: to buy Rentsmith Pro through Google Play.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual records, erase everything (including photos) with Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from the device.

To ask about or request deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat, email support@appsolutedev.com with your purchase receipt details and we will handle it with RevenueCat. Store purchase history itself is kept by Google or Apple under their policies. You can cancel a subscription in Google Play at any time.

Security

Your records are protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and device encryption, and by the operating system keeping each app's storage private. Please use a screen lock, because anyone who can open your phone can open the app.

Your tenants' information

You may store your tenants' names and contact details. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep them, such as managing the tenancy, and for keeping your phone secure. This information never reaches us.

Children

Rentsmith is for adults managing rental property. It is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, POPIA in South Africa, the Australian Privacy Act, PIPEDA in Canada or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to complain to a regulator. Because the app's data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app. For anything else, including the purchase records described above, contact us at support@appsolutedev.com.

Not advice

Rentsmith is a record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice. Compliance reminders are a convenience: check the rules for your area.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it at https://appsolutedev.com/rentsmith/privacy.html and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
