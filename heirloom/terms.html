What Heirloom does

Heirloom identifies antiques, thrift finds and collectibles from your photos, gives an estimated value range, and links to real sold prices. The free plan includes 3 scans a day. Heirloom Pro adds more scans, more photos per scan, a listing generator, an unlimited collection and more. The app shows what each plan includes before you buy.

AI estimates, not appraisals

  • Results are generated by artificial intelligence from your photos. They are estimates for information only, not a formal appraisal, valuation or authentication, and they can be wrong, especially about the maker, age and whether an item is genuine.
  • Value ranges are a guide to what similar items may sell for, not an offer to buy. What an item is really worth depends on its condition, demand, where and how it is sold, and things a photo cannot show.
  • Before you sell, buy, insure, donate or throw away anything that may be valuable, check real sold prices and, for valuable items, get an appraisal from a qualified professional.
  • Sold-price links open eBay, Etsy and Google. Those sites are run by others under their own terms, and we are not responsible for their content or prices. Etsy shows asking prices, not sold prices.
  • Listings from the listing generator (Pro) are drafts. Check them before you publish them. You are responsible for your listings, including their accuracy and following each marketplace's rules.
  • If a result looks wrong or inappropriate, tap Report a problem with this result so we can look at it.

The scanning service

  • Scanning needs an internet connection. Your photos are processed by our server using Google's Gemini API. We aim to keep the service running, but it may sometimes be slow or unavailable, and we may change how it works, including which AI model it uses.
  • The free plan includes 3 scans a day with 1 photo per scan. Pro removes the free daily limit and allows up to 4 photos per scan. A fair-use cap (currently 30 scans a day) still applies to Pro, to prevent abuse and keep the service affordable, and we may change it.
  • Daily limits reset at midnight UTC. A scan that fails because of a problem on our side does not count toward your limit.
