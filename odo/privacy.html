Odo privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Odo ("the app") is published by Appsolute Dev (Appsolute Solutions), South Africa. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your logbook stays on your phone, and we do not run a server that receives it.

Contact: support@appsolutedev.com
Web version: https://appsolutedev.com/odo/privacy.html

What Odo stores, and where

Odo stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your settings: tax authority, distance unit, currency, rate overrides, trip purposes.
  • Vehicles you add: name, registration, type and odometer unit.
  • Odometer readings and their dates.
  • Trips: date and time, from and to, purpose, category, distance, odometer readings, notes, and for GPS trips a simplified route (a few hundred coordinates).
  • Saved places and favourite trips.
  • While a trip is recording, the GPS fixes for that trip, so the trip survives if the app is closed.

There is no Odo account. We (Appsolute Dev) cannot see, access or recover this data.

Location

  • Odo asks for location permission ("while using the app") only when you start your first GPS trip.
  • Location is used only while a trip you started is recording, to calculate the distance on your phone. A notification ("Odo is recording your trip") shows the whole time. Recording stops when you tap Stop.
  • Odo does not request background location and never tracks you between trips.
  • You can use Odo without location at all, by adding trips manually or from your odometer.

Place names (reverse geocoding)

After a GPS trip, Odo can fill in the "from" and "to" fields with street names. To do this, the coordinates of the start and end of the trip are passed to your phone's built-in geocoding service: on Android this is the system Geocoder (provided by Google Play services), on iOS it is Apple's geocoder. Those providers handle the request under their own privacy policies. Odo sends nothing else with it. You can turn this off in Settings ("Look up place names"); trips then keep only the coordinates on your device.

Purchases

Odo Pro is sold through Google Play (and the App Store on iOS). Payments are handled entirely by Google or Apple; Odo never sees your card details. To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service provider, which receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase and subscription records, and basic device information (such as the operating system and app version). RevenueCat processes this on our behalf to manage your subscription. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Exports and sharing

PDF, CSV and JSON exports are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send them using your phone's share sheet (for example email or a cloud drive), under that service's own terms.

What Odo does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in Settings, "Delete all data", or by uninstalling the app. Export a copy first (Settings, "Export all data") if you need it for your records; tax authorities usually expect you to keep logbooks for several years. Purchase records are kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat as required for billing.

Children

Odo is a business and tax tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Because Odo keeps your logbook on your device, you have direct control: you can view, correct, export and delete it in the app. For questions about the limited data handled by RevenueCat on our behalf, or any privacy request under POPIA, the UK or EU GDPR, or other laws, contact support@appsolutedev.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at https://appsolutedev.com/odo/privacy.html with a new "last updated" date, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
